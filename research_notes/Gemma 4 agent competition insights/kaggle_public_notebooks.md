## What do top-claimed notebooks do that ours does not? Is there a "core kicker"?

### Takeaway
No public notebook shows a repeatable edge. Every traced public file set averages about 9.25-10.2/58, the same as our 10 and 9. Four differences between us and the public consensus are worth checking, ranked by evidence: (1) our 120 s `timeout_seconds` also bounds the hidden-test pytest run; (2) we do not pin `{problem_description}` against compaction; (3) we sample at temperature 0.0 / top_p 1.0 with a 40-turn cap, against 0.2 / 0.95 and 80 turns; (4) on thinking budget, 2048 (and our round 7's 1024) is below the public 4096, with mixed evidence. I found no harness exploit, best-of-n or test-running trick.

- **Kicker candidate 1: verification timeout.** In swegemma 0.2.10, `EvalConfig.timeout_seconds` overrides `harness.command_timeout_seconds` (`config.py` lines 178-190), and the hidden-test run in `harness/verification.py` (about line 634) runs `python3 -m pytest <test files>` with `timeout=config.harness.command_timeout_seconds`. `resolved = test_res.exit_code == 0` [V, local source]. leoprovorov says `requests` test files take about 100 s locally, so a correct patch can fail under a short timeout [C] ([Link](https://www.kaggle.com/code/leoprovorov/score-to-gemma-4)). hitarthjain0 says the same: "per command, and also bounds the grader's pytest run" [C] ([Link](https://www.kaggle.com/code/hitarthjain0/gemma-4-agent-what-took-lb-0-12-0-24)). Against this, 60 s bundles have a claimed 0.22 (Repair Lab exp021) [C] ([Link](https://www.kaggle.com/code/denpugovkin/gemma-4-repair-lab-compare-before-you-submit)).
- **Kicker candidate 2: issue pinning.** The harness puts `problem_description` (and `hints`, when present) into ADK session state (`harness/agent_runner.py` line 639-643) [V, local source]. The issue text otherwise lives only in the first user message (`agent_runner.py` around line 340) [V]. mobassir reports about 80% of runs exceed the 14,336-token compaction threshold, and the odds of solving halve after one compaction (OR 0.50, CI 0.35-0.64, controlling for difficulty) [C] ([Link](https://www.kaggle.com/code/mobassir/why-your-leaderboard-can-t-see-a-3-point-gain)). No prompt in our repo contains `{problem_description` [V, grep of the repository]. Use `{hints?}` with the `?`: a missing state key otherwise breaks templating [I, from the ADK convention that the notebooks follow].
- **Thinking budget, mixed evidence:** local 129-task single runs show 2048 → 50 against 4096 → 48 [C]; moranzavdi shows 1024 flat (48/150 each) [C]; dominyellow saw little change from 4096 to 1024 or 512 [C]; mobassir saw halving to 2048 keep solves level and cut timeouts from 10 to 2 [C]. flexonafft's single LB draws were 256 → 0.10, 1024 → about 0.16-0.17 and 4096 → 0.22 (the 0.22 is the lucky V10 draw, with mean 8.71) [C] ([Link](https://www.kaggle.com/code/flexonafft/gemma-4-budget-aware-coding-agent-lean-conte)).
- **Budgets and runtime:** 40 calls at 8 min has timed out at least once on the scorer (alexust), as have 15 min / 50 calls and an 8 min / 240 s variant (dominyellow) [C]. Both of our submissions completed and scored (0.17 and 0.15). v2 is recorded as 8 min / 40 calls / 40 turns / 120 s [V, repo plan docs/45-campaign-plan.md, line 322].
- **Noise ceiling:** pooled per-draw SD 1.56 tasks; 95% of draws within ±3.2 tasks of the mean; detecting a 1-task difference takes about 40 draws per arm [C] ([Link](https://www.kaggle.com/code/dariushafshar/gemma-4-most-run-public-config-averages-10-58)). mobassir gives σ ≈ 1.53 and 18-37 draws per arm [C].

### Inferences
- [I] The most plausible concrete loss in our configuration is candidate 1. Any hidden task whose target test files take more than 120 s (for example on a slower scorer, about 1.6-1.7× slower than an RTX 6000 per alexust) is graded as a failure regardless of the patch. Raising timeout_seconds to 240 costs agent time only when a command runs long. The harness also tells the agent the timeout, and an agent-side `timeout 150 pytest` habit limits the downside. Before acting, check the hidden scorer's swegemma version (0.2.11) for the same line.
- [I] Pinning plus the "Done" exit cost nothing at the 12-hour level, and pinning targets a measured failure mode (compaction). Neither has an isolated LB result. Treat both as cheap, low-risk adds, not as proven gains.
- [I] Lowering thinking to 1024 for round 7 is not contradicted by local evidence. The public direction (higher LB with 4096) rests on single noisy draws, so it is weak evidence against 1024.
- [I] Our 40-turn cap is below everyone's 80, but tool calls and time usually bind first (the 11-16 diff found 7 of 3,476 runs hit the turn cap).

### Gaps
- None of the candidates has a paired local or LB measurement in public material. Validating them needs our own paired local runs.
- Whether the hidden scorer applies the bundle's `timeout_seconds` to verification exactly as swegemma 0.2.10 does is not confirmed by any official text I read.

## Do any notebooks reveal scorer mechanics?

### Takeaway
Yes, and several points are confirmable in the harness source. A task resolves only if pytest on the hidden test files exits 0 within the bundle's `timeout_seconds`. The working tree is diffed against a baseline even without submit_patch. `get_status` and `submit_patch` do not consume tool calls. A low-budget warning attaches when 10 or fewer calls remain. The whole run (about 120 tasks, setup included) must finish within 12 hours, or it shows "exceeded the allowed runtime" and earns no score.

### Cited Findings
- `submit_patch` and `get_status` are decorated `@budget_gated(count_tool_call=False)`, so they are not counted toward `max_tool_calls` [V, `swegemma/tools/execution.py` lines 74-122]. Patchsmith's prompt relies on this ("get_status and submit_patch are free") [C] ([Link](https://www.kaggle.com/code/hanifnoerrofiq/patchsmith-gemma-4-developer-agent)).
- `_attach_low_budget_warning` adds a `budget_warning` field when remaining calls are 10 or fewer, and only if `max_tool_calls` ≥ 20 [V, `swegemma/tools/base.py` line 47]. With 40 calls it starts at call 30 [C, pablodiegoo].
- The harness writes "## Task Budget", "Single command timeout …" and the offline rules into the first user prompt, after the problem statement and hints [V, `agent_runner.py` around lines 300-360]. alexust advises keeping the prompt's numbers consistent with "the budget the harness appends" [C].
- Verification runs `PYTHONSAFEPATH=1 … python3 -s -m pytest <targets> --junitxml … -p no:anyio -o timeout=0`, and a "0 passed / no tests ran / collected 0 items" output is treated as invalid [V, `harness/verification.py`].
- **12-hour limit:** it "covers all ~120 hidden tasks, sandbox setup included, patch validation excluded"; the scorer is estimated at 1.6-1.7× slower than an RTX PRO 6000 running three tasks at a time [C, alexust] ([Link](https://www.kaggle.com/code/alexust/one-bundle-three-outcomes-0-24-0-17-no-score)). One report says a 4.5-min cap took about 10 h scored, implying about 0.5 min per task of overhead [C, via hitarthjain0]. The harness default is 60 min / 500 turns, so the per-task caps come from eval_config [C, hitarthjain0]. lucifer19 instead says the harness default is 50 tool calls [C].
- **Scoring latency and queue:** scoring takes 12-46 h per submission [C, dauntingx]. Since 8 Oct each team may have one pending submission (staff thread 747519) [C, hitarthjain0].
- **Sandbox image:** `docker/Dockerfile.sandbox` uses Python 3.13 and pytest 8.4.2 with the competition wheels, two shims and the repository on the path. Gold patches pass 127 of 129 public tasks in a replica [C, dauntingx] ([Link](https://www.kaggle.com/code/dauntingx/scorer-probe-s25-noread-clean-sandbox)). The hardware is 4×L4 [C, multiple].
- **Context ceiling:** vLLM max_model_len is 32,768, so prompt plus max_output_tokens above it fails [C, leoprovorov]. 75 of 692 local runs ended at the 32k context ceiling [C, pablodiegoo].
- **The public split is 58 tasks** (truncated scores; one task ≈ 0.017) [C, dariushafshar, reproduced by mobassir]. Ties go to the earlier submission; two final selections are allowed [C, citing the rules page].
- Small-model prize board: top public 0.06 among E2B/E4B entrants, data dated 2026-10-20 [V, notebook output] ([Link](https://www.kaggle.com/code/ryanholbrook/gemma-4-developer-agent-small-model-leaderboard)).

### Inferences
- [I] The free `get_status` / `submit_patch` calls mean a prompt can ask for a status check, or an early `submit_patch` snapshot, at no cost in calls. Each still costs a model turn and wall time, and our 40-turn cap makes turns scarcer than in the public 80-turn bundles.
- [I] Since an edit in the tree counts without `submit_patch`, the main scorer-side risks are runtime (12 h) and the verification timeout, not a missing submit.

### Gaps
- The official HARNESS_README and the staff discussion threads (747519, 746250, 742807) were not read directly. Those claims are second-hand.
- No notebook shows the hidden scorer's actual `timeout_seconds` handling, its per-task overhead or its GPU speed. All of these are estimates.
